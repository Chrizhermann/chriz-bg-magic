# Decided spell batches: checks, 2026-11-06

What was checked for the three merged batches (vanilla, SCS-based, kept ideas) and what was not.

## Install checks

- Offline tests (`tests/run_tests.py`, WeiDU 249, made-up games): 24 of 24 passed.
- Real installs with WeiDU on real data, in two disposable labs: BG2:EE 2.7.3.0 + EEex v1.3.1, and
  EET with EE Fixpack: `SUCCESSFULLY INSTALLED`, no warnings in either.
- EE Fixpack keeps some cures in sub-spells of its own (`#curepsn` and others). Neutralize Poison
  refused that layout at first; it accepts it now, and an offline test covers it.

Two independent read-only reviews compared the real install output with the rows and with the
behaviour of SCS 35.21 and Spell Revisions, a third checked the merged result. Their findings are
fixed, with one exception: Lesser Restoration's cure of attribute drain was taken out instead.
Extracting drain effects into spells of their own rewrote about 70 spells and items, changed
probabilities and broke existing cures. It comes back through EEex, without rewriting files.

## In-game checks (lab A)

`tests/lab/run_batches.py` drove a hidden, muted lab game (same lab, test party and arena as the
earlier rounds; `tests/lab/probe_batches.lua` ran inside the game). 29 checks passed; the guards
stayed clean. Nothing was saved.

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Strength of One | Strength 10 becomes 18/75; 19 stays 19; 18/90 stays 18/90 |
| Magic Resistance | 50 becomes 86 (level-18 caster); a second casting changes nothing |
| Reflected Image, then Mirror Image, then Reflected Image | the second replaces the first; the third does nothing |
| Mordenkainen's Sword | summoned; Disintegrate destroys it, with the best save too |
| Faerie Fire (new projectile) | enemy −2 AC; party untouched |
| Mass Regenerate, Invisibility Sphere (new projectiles) | reach a party member other than the caster, spare the enemy |
| Stinking Cloud, Spook, Horror | a skeleton spared, a tasloi caught |
| Spook | lands without True Seeing, not with it |
| Insect Plague | spell failure and damage with the worst saves; nothing under Fire Shield |
| Nature's Beauty | made save: blind for a short time, alive; failed save on a non-humanoid: blind for good; failed save on a humanoid: dead |
| Cacofiend, Summon Fiend, Gate | a fiend appears (lawful caster: Bone Fiend, Cornugon, Pit Fiend) |
| Smoke test | 113 changed spells cast once at a held creature; the game kept answering |

## Not checked

- **Area spells for the whole party.** In this arena a party-only area spell reaches only the
  member at the target point. The base game's Haste does the same, so this comes from the lab.
  Strength of One was cast on each member in turn; whether Mass Regenerate and Invisibility Sphere
  reach several members at once is open.
- **Fiends in combat.** Spawned creatures do not act in this arena: the base game's Pit Fiend, whose
  script turns hostile on sight, stayed idle for 20 seconds just like ours. Their scripts compile and
  read back as written; their behaviour in a fight is untested.
- Chaotic and neutral casters for the fiend spells.
- Contingency, the sequencers and Find Familiar were left out of the smoke test: they open a
  dialogue or need the main character.
- Everything in EET beyond the install.
